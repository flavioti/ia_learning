[[LLM model]]

An LLM cut-off (or [[knowledge cut-off]]) is the final date up to which a large language model was fed training data.

> [!NOTE] TLDR
> The date of data used to train the model

## How to Bypass It

- **[[LLM Web Search]]:** Many modern AI platforms use tools like Google Search to look up live information. ([[MCP]], [[Tools]])

- **[[RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)]]:** Systems connect the AI to private databases or live web pages to fetch fresh facts.
