#ModelOptimization

Is the process to taking an existing, pre-trained AI model and training it futher on a smaller, specific dataset to teach it a particular skill, style, or subject.
Instead of building a large language model from scratch, wich costs a lot of time and money, developers use a general foundation model as a starting point and adjust its internal settings (weight and parameters) to make it an expert in areas like medical diagnosis, legal writing, or coding.

It ==changes the model behavior==, by changing weights permanently, intead of RAG that changes the model's knowledge context.
