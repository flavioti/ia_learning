#open-source #data-visualization #analytics #monitoring #dashboard

Is an open-source **data visualization and analytics platform**. 
- Dashboards: It transforms raw data into rich, interactive graphs, charts, tables, and heatmaps.
- It does not store metrics itself; instead, it connects to external databases like [[Prometheus]], [[InfluxDB]], [[MySQL]], or [[AWS CloudWatch 1]] to pull and display information.
- Lets teams combine metrics from different systems into a single, clean dashboard to spot performance bottlenecks or failures quickly.
