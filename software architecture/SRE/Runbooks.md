A runbook is a step-by-step guide for operating a running service: routine tasks, such as restarting a component, and responses to alerts or incidents. Playbook is often used as a synonym, especially for incident response.

Runbooks are written for production operation, not to plan a change. For the design side, see [[llm/system design/Design Docs.md|Design Docs]].
