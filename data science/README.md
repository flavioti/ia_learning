# Data Science

## Definition

**Data Science** is the field of extracting **knowledge and decisions from data**. It combines statistics, programming and domain knowledge to collect, clean, analyze and model data, and to communicate the results.

>[!tip] Turn raw data into insights, predictions and decisions.

## Typical workflow

1. **Define the problem:** what question or decision are we supporting?
2. **Collect data:** databases, APIs, files, logs.
3. **Clean and prepare:** fix missing values and errors. See [[Data Cleaning]] and [[Feature Engineering]].
4. **Explore:** statistics and visualization to understand the data.
5. **Model:** apply [[Machine Learning]] or statistical methods.
6. **Evaluate:** measure the quality of the results. See [[Model Evaluation]].
7. **Communicate and deploy:** share insights, or put the model into production.
