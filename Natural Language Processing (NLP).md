LP is a field of study focused on the interaction between computers and human language.

1. **Language understanding**: NLP models can analyze and understand natural language text, including its syntax, semantics, and pragmatics.
2. **Text generation**: NLP models can generate human-like text, including summaries, translations, and creative writing.
3. **Question answering**: NLP models can answer questions based on the content of a given text or database.
4. **Sentiment analysis**: NLP models can analyze the emotional tone of text, including sentiment, emotion, and intent.
5. **Language translation**: NLP models can translate text from one language to another.
6. **Speech recognition**: NLP models can recognize and transcribe spoken language.
7. **Dialogue systems**: NLP models can generate and respond to human-like dialogue.
