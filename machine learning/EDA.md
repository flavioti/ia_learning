Is the initial phase to inspect, summarize and visualize a dataset

- Structural inspection: Verify the number of rows, columns, data types (numerical, categorical, dates), and missing or duplicate values.
- Univariante analysis: Calculate measures of central tendency and dispersion (mean, median, standard deviation) for numeric data, and frequency tables for categorical data.
- Bivariate and multivariate analysis: Explore relationships between variables using correlation matrices, scatter plots, and feature importance analysis.
- Anomaly detection: Identify outliers and determine whether they are data collection errors or important real-word signals.
