> [!tip] Is a numeric representation of text, image and audio, a [[vector]] that specifically compresses and encodes semantic meaning.

These are long lists of numbers (often 768 or 1,536 dimensions) generated by AI models. They capture the deep semantic meaning and context of data, which vector databases store and search.

- Semantic Meaning: Geometric distance reflects real-world relationships.
- Primary Context: [[Artificial Inteligence]], [[NLP]] ([[LLM]]), and [[Machine Learning]].
- How it's made: Generated by training algorithms or neural networks.
