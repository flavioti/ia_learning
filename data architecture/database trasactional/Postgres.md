[[OLTP Database]]
## The Evolution of the Name

- **POSTGRES (1986):** The project started at UC Berkeley as the successor to an older database called INGRES. The name simply stood for **"Post-Ingres"**. At this time, it used a query language called **QUEL**.
- **Postgres95 (1995):** Two graduate students replaced the old QUEL language with **SQL**. To highlight this massive upgrade, they updated the name to Postgres95.
- **PostgreSQL (1996):** To give the project a timeless name that didn't include a specific year, the core team chose **PostgreSQL** to permanently reflect its open-source **SQL** standards.

Because "PostgreSQL" can be difficult to pronounce, the community officially adopted "Postgres" as its official alias.
