#llm #model [[decision model]]

Is a proprietary [[System One]] artificial intelligence model developed by [[TypeSafe AI]] that outputs structured decisions, probabilities, and confidence scores instead of writing natural-language text
- **Core Function:** Unlike large language models (LLMs) like GPT or Claude that generate text word-by-word, Jev acts like an advanced, intelligent decision-making switch.
- **Inputs & Outputs:** You give it context (text, JSON, or data) alongside predefined questions with bounded answer choices. It returns typed values and probabilities for those choices rather than open-ended prose.
- **System One Thinking:** The name reflects Daniel Kahneman’s psychological concept of fast, intuitive "System 1" thinking rather than slow, deliberate "System 2" reasoning

Training method: [[Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions (RLCD)]]
