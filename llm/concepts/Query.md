#machine

> [!tip] Is the technical, structural term. The moment you hit "send", LLM's backend takes your [[prompt]], bundles it with your chat history, converts it into numbers (tokens/vectors), and runs a **query** (a computational request) against the database and mathematical weights of the AI model to calculate what the response should be.

In everyday conversation, at work, or when discussing how to talk to AI, use the word [[prompt]]. If you are writing code to connect to the [[LLM API]] or building a [[Vector Search]] database ([[RAG]]), that is when you would use the word query.

query = [[prompt]] + chat history
