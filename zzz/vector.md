To understand how it works, imagine we want to represent words using simple 3-number vectors based on three traits: Animalness, Fluffiness, Size. The values range from -1.0 (low) to 1.0 (high).
- **Dog**: [0.9, 0.8, -0.2] (Highly animal, highly fluffy, relatively small)
- **Cat**: [0.9, 0.9, -0.6] (Highly animal, very fluffy, very small)
- **Car**: [-0.9, -0.9, 0.8] (Not a animal, not fluffy, quiet large)
Because the numbers for **Dog** and **Cat** are very similar, their vectors sit close together in this mathematical space. The vector for **Car** is completely different, placing it far away.
